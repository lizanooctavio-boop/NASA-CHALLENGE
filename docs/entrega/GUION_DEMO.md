# Demo Script & Slide Deck Plan / Guion del Vídeo y Diapositivas (EN & ES)

**Official NASA Space Apps 2026 Demo Limits:**
- **Option A — Video Demo:** Maximum **30 seconds (`<= 30 s`)**, hosted on a public URL with mandatory **English subtitles** (and English narration recommended).
- **Option B — Slide Deck:** Maximum **7 slides (`<= 7 slides`, including title slide)**, written entirely in **English**, hosted on a public URL.
- **Opening (recommended by the official guide):** team name and the challenge in the first ~5 seconds; keep the whole video at 30 seconds or less.
- **No minors:** nobody under 18 may appear or be heard in the video.
- **Required Walkthrough Sequence:**
  1. **3D Globe (`Globe`)** →
  2. **`Investigate` (`202` series across `74` places & `8` categories)** →
  3. **`Trend detected` case:** Mediterranean marine heatwaves (`+22.60 days/decade`, $p = 0.005$) & SST (`+0.372 °C/decade`, $p < 0.001$) →
  4. **`Methods disagree` case:** North Atlantic hurricane rapid intensification (`+2.105 steps/decade`, HAC CI excludes zero while AR(1)-OLS CI `[-0.215, +4.924]` and Mann–Kendall $p = 0.064$ cross 5%) →
  5. **Forest loss in Nicaragua (Landsat 30 m / Hansen GFC v1.12):** `20,231 km²` lost in `2001–2024` (`26%` of year-2000 forest; `+255.8 km²/decade`, `Methods disagree` nationally; `-14.2 km²/decade`, `Trend detected` in Pacific lowlands) →
  6. **`Sources` (`13/13` challenge resources + open datasets & CSV/JSON export)**.
