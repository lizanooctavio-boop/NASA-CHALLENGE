## Slide 2 — Why Single Tests Fail & Our 4-Method Verdict Engine

- **Content:**
  - **The Statistical Trap (Real Project Example — Gulf of Fonseca Chlorophyll-a, 2003–2024):**
    - Naive monthly OLS over 264 months (NTRS method): **`+1.21 mg/m³/decade`, `p = 0.0007`** (looks highly significant!).
    - Deseasonalized annual series + autocorrelation control: **`+0.68 mg/m³/decade`, Modified Mann–Kendall `p = 0.08`** -> **`Methods disagree`** (and sensor bias fails `≤ 1 mg/m³` criterion).
  - **Why Rainfall Needs Caution:** Tropical rainfall correlates strongly with El Niño (GPM IMERG Jun–Nov vs. ONI: **`r = −0.561`** in Nicaragua, `n = 24` yrs), creating wide year-to-year swings that mask moderate slopes.
  - **Our Statistical Guardrail (`backend/clima_nic/estadistica/` & `tendencia.py`):**
    1. **OLS with effective degrees of freedom** (`n_ef`, Santer et al., 2000)
    2. **OLS with Newey–West HAC errors** (Conley, 1999)
    3. **Non-parametric Theil–Sen slope** (`95% CI`) + sensitivity (`±3 yrs`, leave-one-year-out)
    4. **Modified Mann–Kendall** (`Hamed & Rao, 1998`) + **descriptive Pettitt (1979) step-change detector** + **Benjamini–Hochberg FDR (`q = 0.10`)** for gridded maps.
    - **Honest rule:** *"Not enough evidence of a trend"* **≠** *"no change"* (protects against Type II error).
- **Suggested Visual:**
  - Left: side-by-side comparison card from our *Learn* tab (`p = 0.0007` naive vs. `p = 0.08` adjusted). Right: 3-outcome verdict funnel showing consensus requirement across all 3 CIs + MK `p < 0.05`.
- **Speaker Notes:**
  - Explain how we prevent p-hacking and autocorrelation inflation. Even when tests yield *"Not enough evidence"*, we explicitly educate users in the *Learn* tab not to mistake statistical uncertainty for zero physical risk.

## Slide 3 — Core Demonstration: Nicaragua vs. Canadian Prairies (`2001–2024`)

- **Content:**
  - **Why Two Contrasting Regions?** Guided walkthrough **`Nicaragua vs. Canadian Prairies`** in our **Investigate** tab compares the same `2001–2024` satellite/station era across two starkly different climates:
  - **Air Temperature (`NASA GISTEMP v4`, 2001–2024):**
    - **Nicaragua (`~119,900 km²`):** **`+0.40 °C/decade`** (95% CI `+0.21 to +0.54`, MMK `p < 0.001`) -> **`Trend detected`** (44-yr `1981–2024`: `+0.29 °C/decade`).
    - **Canadian Prairies (`467,128 km²`, AAFC ecozone):** **`+0.18 °C/decade`** (95% CI `−0.57 to +0.75`, MMK `p = 0.637`, range `−0.29 to +2.68 °C`) -> **`Not enough evidence of a trend`**. *Same dataset, higher mid-latitude variance hides the slope over 24 years.*
  - **Annual Rainfall (`NASA GPM IMERG Final V07B`, 2001–2024):**
    - **Nicaragua:** Mean `2,094 mm`, Sen `−64.9 mm/decade` (CI `−233 to +98`, MK `p = 0.442`) -> **`Not enough evidence`**. *(Contrast: Costa Rica shows `MCO −201 / Sen −168 mm/dec` -> **`Methods disagree`**).*
    - **Canadian Prairies:** Mean `435.2 mm/yr`, Sen `−30.4 mm/decade` (May–Sep `−20.2 mm/dec`) -> **`Not enough evidence`**, cross-checked against 3 NOAA GSOM Saskatchewan stations (`r = 0.830` rainfall, `r = 0.961` temperature).
- **Suggested Visual:**
  - Direct capture of the **Investigate** tab guided comparison panel showing Nicaragua (`Trend detected`, coral badge) side-by-side with Canadian Prairies (`Not enough evidence`, neutral badge) plus the annual IMERG vs. GSOM comparison plot.
- **Speaker Notes:**
  - Walk judges through the exact contrast: Nicaragua shows statistically clear warming in 24 years, while the Canadian Prairies experience almost `3 °C` interannual swings that produce an inconclusive CI despite positive point estimates.

## Slide 4 — Cross-Checks as Clues: When Sources Agree vs. Disagree

- **Content:**
  - **Principle:** *Disagreement between independent datasets is a diagnostic finding, not something to hide.*
  - **Case 1 — Surface Soil Moisture (`0–10 cm`, 2001–2024):**
    - **GLDAS-2.1 Noah** (GES DISC Cloud OPeNDAP, `0.7–2.6%` water-balance residual): Nicaragua `−0.0071 m³/m³/dec` (*Methods disagree*); Central Nicaragua `−0.0090 m³/m³/dec` and Canadian Prairies `−0.0139 m³/m³/dec` (*Trend detected*, with Pettitt breaks around 2011–2013 & AGRMET forcing caveat).
    - **MERRA-2 `GWETTOP`:** Opposite sign in Nicaragua (`+0.048/dec`), near-zero correlation (`r = −0.03 to 0.36`).
  - **Case 2 — Temperature & Precipitation Cross-Checks (`Nicaragua`):**
    - **Temperature (`1981–2024`):** GISTEMP v4 **`+0.29 °C/decade`** (*Trend detected*) vs. NASA POWER/MERRA-2 **`−0.11 °C/decade`** (*Not enough evidence*, Pettitt step jump in `1999`, `p = 0.0077`).
    - **Rainfall (`2001–2024`):** CRU TS 4.10 (`−21.3 mm/dec`, `r = 0.63`, active stations drop `3.7 -> 1.8` after `2015`); GPCC v2025 (`−79.2 mm/dec`, `r = 0.94` — matches IMERG closely because GPCC gauges calibrate IMERG); local GHCN-D rainfall records halt around `1989–1991` (2003 max).
- **Suggested Visual:**
  - Two clean side-by-side **Cross-checks** charts captured from *Investigate*: left panel showing `GLDAS-2.1` vs `MERRA-2` opposite soil-moisture curves; right panel showing `GISTEMP v4` vs `MERRA-2/POWER` divergence + Pettitt shift callout.
- **Speaker Notes:**
  - Highlight why this directly fulfills *"Earth System Trend Detective"*: instead of cherry-picking whichever source yields a steep line, the app exposes gauge dropouts (CRU post-2015), assimilation breaks (POWER 1999/2005), and forcing shifts (GLDAS AGRMET).

## Slide 5 — Lakes, SAR Radar Demonstrations & 2050 Climate Scenarios

- **Content:**
  - **Lake Altimetry Cross-Validation (Lakes Cocibolca & Xolotlán):**
    - **G-REALM (`1993–2025`, 33 full yrs):** Lake Cocibolca `+0.11 m/decade` (95% CI `−0.01 to +0.23`) -> **`Not enough evidence`**.
    - **SWOT Hydrocron (`2023–present`):** `41` overlapping months correlate with G-REALM at **`r = 0.99`** (bias `−0.437 m`, `sd = 0.073 m`). SWOT (`~40–41 mo`) is explicitly tagged **`Record too short for a trend test`**.
  - **Radar Surface-Water Demo (`OPERA RTC-S1`, `OPERA DSWx-S1`, `CSA Tutorial`):**
    - `75` Sentinel-1 RTC bursts + `37` DSWx-S1 optical-calibrated tiles (`2,585.25 km²` common water = `33.05%` of lake polygon). Radar thresholding is gated as **`NO_VALIDADA`** until independent ground truth confirms accuracy (`RCM EODMS Order #3787605` submitted for follow-up processing).
  - **Nicaragua Future Projections (`NASA NEX-GDDP-CMIP6 v2.0`, 6 GCMs, `2041–2060` vs. `1995–2014`):**
    - Warming median: **`+1.40 °C`** (SSP2-4.5) and **`+1.98 °C`** (SSP5-8.5, `6/6` models warmer).
    - Rainfall median: **`−5.45%`** (SSP2-4.5) and **`−10.49%`** (SSP5-8.5). *(Exploratory MODIS site NDVI shows greenness drop `−0.043/dec` in Central site sample, caveated as single-site without regional mask).*
- **Suggested Visual:**
  - Top: Lake Cocibolca SWOT vs. G-REALM tight monthly overlay (`r = 0.99`). Bottom: Nicaragua 2050 CMIP6 scenario summary cards + clear **`Short Record / Demo Only`** honesty badges.
