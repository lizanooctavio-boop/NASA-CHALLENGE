## 1. Summary Disclosure (Ready to Paste into the Submission Form)

> **Use of AI / Super Intelligence (SI) Tools:**  
> Our human team defined the scientific questions, statistical verdict criteria, dataset selection, regional domain design, and quality-control rules, and used AI coding and research assistants (**Claude Code / Claude Opus**, **OpenAI Codex**, and **Google Gemini / Antigravity**) to accelerate Python data-ingestion scripts, statistical test modules, frontend JavaScript/CSS components, literature synthesis drafts, and English/Spanish documentation. No numerical values, slopes, confidence intervals, or verdicts were generated by AI: every series in the application is computed deterministically from raw public satellite, station, and reanalysis archives by `backend/clima_nic` and verified through **the full automated `pytest` suite**, **independent recalculation of time series from primary sources**, and **automated Crossref API verification (`api.crossref.org`) of every published DOI**. Neither the application nor any demo asset uses or modifies official NASA, CSA, or NOAA logos, insignias, or mission emblems.

## 2. Division of Responsibility: Human Team vs. AI Assistants

### What the Human Team Directed and Decided
1. **Scientific & Statistical Architecture:**
   - Designed the four-estimator consensus rule (**Theil–Sen slope**, **modified Mann–Kendall with Hamed–Rao 1998 autocorrelation correction**, **AR(1)-adjusted Ordinary Least Squares**, and **Newey–West HAC**) and the three-verdict taxonomy (`Trend detected`, `Not enough evidence of a trend`, `Methods disagree`), plus the `< 10` years short-record guardrail (`Record too short for a trend test`) and `Not validated` (`NO_VALIDADA`) SAR tag.
   - Established the strict rule that **only the statistical engine (`tendencia_robusta.analizar`) can assign verdicts**—never a language model or a pre-written table.
2. **Dataset Vetting & Scientific Audits:**
   - Audited every regional time series and rejected or corrected physically misleading series before merging into `codex/herramientas-nasa`:
     - Removed satellite radar altimetry trend testing on **Lake Poopó** (Bolivia) because radar altimetry over 2015–2016 measures the dry salt flat bed (`+0.13 m/decade` artifact on a lake that dried out), removing the Lake Poopó series from the app (Landsat-based **JRC Global Surface Water** extent is used instead for Lakes Nicaragua and Managua) and requiring $\ge 12$ valid observations per year on 10-day G-REALM records.
     - Separated **Colorado River at Lees Ferry (USGS 09380000)** into the full `1922–2025` record (`Trend detected`, `-0.412 km³/decade`) and the post-Glen Canyon Dam regulated period `1963–2025` (`Not enough evidence`, `-0.052 km³/decade`).
     - Exposed reanalysis step jumps in **NASA POWER / MERRA-2** over Nicaragua (Pettitt breaks in 1999 and 2005) and optical turbidity interference in **Gulf of Fonseca MODIS-Aqua chlorophyll-a** (`7.02 mg/m³` vs. `0.44 mg/m³` in the open Pacific).
3. **UI/UX & Visual Integrity:**
   - Built upon the team's prior open-weather viewer architecture (`SICC-952`, declared transparently as prior work) and reviewed all UI views, interactive charts, and guided tours.

### What Was Done with AI / SI Tools
- **Data-Ingestion & Processing Scripts (`data/recursos/` and `backend/clima_nic/`):** AI coding assistants (**Claude Code / Opus**, **Codex**, and **Gemini / Antigravity**) helped write and refactor Python downloaders, parsers, and area-weighted aggregators for NASA GPM IMERG, GISTEMP v4, GLDAS-2.1, MODIS, SMAP, SWOT, OPERA DSWx-S1, G-REALM, NOAA OISST v2.1, ERSSTv5, IBTrACS v04r01, CHIRPS v2.0, CRU TS 4.10, GPCC, USGS NWIS, USDA SNOTEL, NSIDC, GTN-P/PANGAEA, GlaMBIE, GRACE CSR Mascons, Hansen GFC v1.12, and JRC Global Surface Water.
- **Regional Literature Briefs (`docs/reto/recursos/`):** AI assistants helped draft regional climate case-study briefs for the Americas & Caribbean (`investigacion_americas_caribe.md`) and Europe, Africa & Middle East (`investigacion_europa_africa_oriente_medio.md`).
- **Automated Testing & Documentation (`tests/`, `docs/`, `README.md`):** AI assistants helped write unit tests, browser E2E checks, and English/Spanish submission documentation.

## 3. How AI Outputs Were Verified

Every piece of code, numerical series, and bibliographic reference touched by an AI assistant underwent three independent verification layers:

### 3.1 Automated Test Suite
Running `python -m pytest -q` runs the full suite (all tests must pass before any merge):
- **Cryptographic Provenance (`test_json_versionado_esta_al_dia`):** Computes the `SHA-256` hash of every input CSV and JSON file in `data/` and verifies that `data/recursos_integrados.json` matches a fresh deterministic build from `clima_nic.recursos.construir()`.
- **No Synthetic Data Guardrail (`test_rechaza_datos_sinteticos_sin_permiso` & `test_el_json_versionado_no_tiene_datos_sinteticos`):** Rejects any input marked `"sintetico": true` from entering production and asserts that the versioned JSON contains zero synthetic series.
- **Statistical Engine Unit Tests (`test_datos.py`, `test_indicadores_nicaragua.py`):** Verifies OLS, AR(1) effective degrees of freedom, Newey–West HAC confidence intervals, Theil–Sen slopes, Hamed–Rao modified Mann–Kendall $p$-values, Pettitt change-point detection, and Benjamini–Hochberg FDR against analytical and reference benchmarks.
- **End-to-End Browser Verification (`test_e2e_navegador.py`, `test_recursos_interfaz.py`):** Validates JavaScript syntax (`node --check`), DOM rendering, interactive selection, and exact agreement between UI cards, downloadable CSVs, and backend JSON files.

### 3.2 Independent Recalculation of Time Series from Primary Sources
Before merging regional branches (`codex/impl-americas` and `codex/impl-europa-africa`) into `codex/herramientas-nasa`, an independent audit recalculated the annual time series and trend statistics directly from the raw data files:
- Confirmed exact numerical agreement on **Colorado River discharge** (`1922–2025`: `-0.4123 km³/decade`, $p = 0.0029$), **Deadhorse permafrost MAGT** (`1978–2016`: `+0.8333 °C/decade`, $p < 0.0001$), **Lake Superior ice cover at Bayfield** (`1858–2019`: `-3.1915 days/decade`, $p < 0.0001$), **North Atlantic rapid intensification steps** (`1980–2024`: `+2.1053 steps/decade`, `Methods disagree`), **Mediterranean SST** (`1982–2025`: `+0.3723 °C/decade`, $p < 0.0001$), **Central European glaciers** (`2000–2023`: `-0.48 m w.e./decade`, $p = 0.0273$), and **Nicaragua tree cover loss** (`2001–2024`: `20,231 km²` total loss, `+255.78 km²/decade`, `Methods disagree`).

### 3.3 Automated Crossref API Verification of Every DOI
LLMs can hallucinate or conflate bibliographic citations and DOIs. To prevent any fabricated or misattributed reference from entering the app or reports:
- Every `doi:10...` string in `recursos.py`, `recursos_americas.py`, `recursos_europa_africa.py`, and the research reports was queried directly against `https://api.crossref.org/works/<doi>` to verify that the **DOI resolves**, and that **author, year, journal, and title match the claim**.
- **Corrections Caught and Applied by This Audit:**
  1. In `docs/reto/recursos/investigacion_europa_africa_oriente_medio.md`, Crossref verification caught that `doi:10.1016/j.gloplacha.2017.12.014` had been cited with a mixed title; it was corrected to its exact Crossref metadata: **Nicholson, S. E., Funk, C., & Fink, A. H. (2018)**, *"Rainfall over the African continent from the 19th through the 21st century,"* *Global and Planetary Change*, 165, 114–127.
  2. In the same report, the summary of **Lorenz et al. (2019)** (`doi:10.1029/2019GL082062`) was corrected so that **94%** accurately refers to the fraction of ECA&D stations that warmed in summer heat extremes (`+0.33 °C/decade` on average), rather than claiming that heatwave days tripled at 94% of stations.
  3. Unverified preprints (such as `10.5194/sp-2024-4`) and model-only projections mistaken for observations were removed and documented in `docs/reto/recursos/correcciones_investigacion_europa_africa.md`.
